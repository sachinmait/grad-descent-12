The Foggy Mountain

Imagine you are hiking on a mountain in a thick fog. You want to get to the very bottom (the lowest point).

You can't see the bottom, but you can feel the slope under your feet.

The Algorithm

  1. Check the slope at your current position.
  2. Take a step in the opposite direction of the slope.
  3. Repeat until the slope is flat (you are at the bottom).
